01 / The unseenA truth worth saying out loud
Everyone asks about the baby.
Who asks about her?

They call her “amazing.”
But do they see the hard part?

After birth, the world can be quick to praise a mother and slow to ask how she is doing. There are sleepless nights, heavy feelings, and quiet moments when even saying “I need help” feels hard.

生产之后，大家急着称赞妈妈“伟大”，却很少认真问她过得怎么样。那些深夜、疲惫和说不出口的情绪，也值得被看见。

01 / THE LABEL

“You're so strong.”

Sometimes praise becomes an expectation to never say how hard it is.

02 / THE NIGHT

“Just keep going.”

There are hours no one sees and feelings she cannot always explain.

03 / THE PERSON

“How are you?”

A small, honest question can make room for a real answer.

Motherhood is not a performance. A mother is allowed to be a person.

03 / A LETTER
TO EVERY MOM

You do not owe anyone a perfect version of yourself.

You can love your child and still feel overwhelmed. You can be grateful and still need rest. You can be a good mother and still ask someone to hold you up for a while.

We made HUMAMA for every mother who has quietly wondered if anyone sees her. We do.

你可以爱孩子，也可以感到疲惫；你可以心怀感激，也可以需要休息。你不必一个人扛下所有。HUMAMA 献给每一位曾怀疑自己是否被看见的妈妈——我们看见你了。

With care, HUMAMA
